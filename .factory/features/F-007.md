# F-007: Show the result, highlight the winning line and play again

- **Milestone:** M3 · **Size:** S · **Requirements:** R-004, R-005, R-006 · **Depends on:** F-006
- **Screens:** `design/screens/game-03-game-over.html` (3a, 3b, 3c)
- **Components:** status line (result), winning-square style with a strike line, "Play again" button
- **Branch:** `factory/F-007-game-over`, stacked on `factory/F-006-render-and-pointer-play` (PR #7)

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given a move that ends the game, when it is applied, then all squares are disabled and the visible result text is "Computer wins with {LINE}.", "It's a draw." or "You win with {LINE}.".
2. Given a computer win, when the result shows, then exactly the three winning squares carry `data-winning="true"` and a `data-line` of `row`, `column`, `diagonal-down` or `diagonal-up`. Each differs from non-winning squares in a non-colour computed style (a 4 px border instead of 2 px) and carries the strike line (a `::after` whose computed `content` is not `none`). Given a draw, then no square is marked.
3. Given a finished game, when the result shows, then "Play again" is visible. During play and while choosing, it is not.
4. Given a finished game, when "Play again" is activated, then the board is empty and the first-mover choice is shown. Its focus rule (focus on "You go first") belongs to F-008 with the other focus rules. Here, "Play again" is a native button in the action area.

## Out of scope

- Moving focus to "Play again" when the game ends, and to "You go first" after it (F-008).
- The live region (F-009).

## Changes

- **`src/ui/view.ts`:**
  - When the phase is `over` with a win, set `data-winning="true"` and `data-line` on the three line squares, and remove both attributes from every square otherwise, keeping the render contract.
  - `classifyLine(line)` returns `row | column | diagonal-down | diagonal-up`, by squares.
  - The action area holds `button.button.button--play-again` "Play again" while `over`, the choice while `choosing`, and nothing while `playing`.
  - A new handler, `onPlayAgain`.
- **`src/main.ts`:** `onPlayAgain` → `apply(resetToChoosing())`, which renders "Who goes first?" and the empty, disabled board.
- **`src/styles.css`:** from the mockup:
  - winning squares: win-surface fill and a 4 px win-marker border
  - a `::after` strike (6 px) oriented per `data-line`
  - a win-surface patch behind the mark so O stays readable
  - "Play again" full width of the action area

## Tests to write first (`tests/e2e/game-over.spec.ts`, all 5 projects, real opponent)

- **3a diagonal win** (human squares 0, 1, 3):
  - the status reads "Computer wins with the diagonal from top right to bottom left."
  - squares 2, 4 and 6 have `data-winning="true"` and `data-line="diagonal-up"`; the other 6 have neither attribute
  - winning squares have a computed `border-top-width` of `4px` and the rest `2px`
  - each winning square's `::after` computed `content` is not `none`
  - all 9 squares are natively disabled
- **3c row win** (human squares 0, 6, 1; O replies 4, 3, 5): squares 3, 4 and 5 have `data-line="row"`, and the status is "Computer wins with row 2.".
- **3b draw** (human squares 0, 1, 6, 5, 8): "It's a draw." and no square has `data-winning`.
- **Play again visibility:** hidden while choosing, hidden during play, visible after a win and after a draw.
- **Play again resets (render contract regression):** after the 3a win, activating "Play again" shows the status "Who goes first?", both choice buttons, 9 empty and disabled squares, and no `data-winning`, `data-line` or `aria-disabled` attributes left on any square. Then "You go first" gives an active empty board, and a move plays normally (X at 0, O at 4).

## Approach

The view stays a pure function of state. The winning line comes from `state.result.line`, never from the DOM. Activation uses the F-006 touch-aware helper (tap or click).
