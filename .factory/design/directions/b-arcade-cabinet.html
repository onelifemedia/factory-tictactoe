Arcade Cabinet

An arcade cabinet after dark: pixel marks, neon on deep ink, a loud marquee that dares you to go again.

Same sample game on every board: you took row 1, columns 1 and 2; the computer blocked at row 1, column 3. In the game-over state the computer has won on the diagonal. Copy is the exact tested copy.

Type

Title: system-ui 900, uppercase, wide tracking, with a hard two-step offset shadow like a cabinet marquee. Status: ui-monospace 700, like a score readout. No font files.

Shape

Hard and square: 0 radius, 3 px cells with a bevel (light top edge, dark bottom edge), 8-bit pixel marks on a 10 × 10 grid. Buttons are chunky arcade buttons with a thick base.

Motion

Stepped, never smooth: the new mark pops in over three frames (240 ms, steps(3)); the winning cells blink twice. Reduced motion: no pop, no blink.

Details

The status reads like a high-score line. “Play again” is framed as “INSERT COIN”-style energy but keeps the exact copy.
