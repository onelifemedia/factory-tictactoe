Tabletop Tiles

Chunky wooden game pieces on a warm table: bright, sure of itself, every square feels like it would click when you press it.

Same sample game on every board: you took row 1, columns 1 and 2; the computer blocked at row 1, column 3. In the game-over state the computer has won on the diagonal. Copy is the exact tested copy.

Type

Title: ui-rounded 800, big and friendly (falls back to system-ui). Body and status: system-ui 600. No font files.

Shape

Chunky and physical: 18 px radius tiles with a 6 px wooden edge underneath, so each tile stands proud of the table. Marks are fat rounded pieces, not glyphs.

Motion

Tactile: tiles sink 3 px when pressed, a new piece drops in with a small bounce (280 ms), and the winning tiles lift. Reduced motion: no drop, no lift; the press is kept as a colour change.

Details

The only light direction of the three. Tomato and teal avoid generic web-app blue; the gold winning tiles carry a dark bar and border too, so colour is never the only cue.
