Chalkboard Night

A game-night chalkboard: hand-drawn marks, dusty colour, a friendly scrawl that still reads at a glance.

Same sample game on every board: you took row 1, columns 1 and 2; the computer blocked at row 1, column 3. In the game-over state the computer has won on the diagonal. Copy is the exact tested copy.

Type

Title: ui-rounded 800, tilted −2° like a scrawled heading (falls back to system-ui). Body and status: system-ui 600. No font files.

Shape

Soft, slightly irregular: squares with 14 px radius and a 2 px chalk-dash edge, marks drawn as uneven strokes with round caps. The board sits in a wooden-frame border.

Motion

Marks draw themselves stroke by stroke (260 ms); the winning line is swiped across in chalk (420 ms). Nothing moves with reduced motion; marks simply appear.

Details

The keyboard hint is written like a chalk footnote. “Play again” is a chalk-white slab that presses down 2 px.
