## Findings
- [C1] [HIGH] `.github/workflows/deploy.yml:131–159,203–206` — Partial reruns can still overwrite a newer deployment through rollback — Example: P passes deploy/smoke; A deploys, smoke fails, rollback fails before uploading; B deploys but its smoke is cancelled, leaving B live. [Re-running failed jobs](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/how-tos/manage-workflow-runs/re-run-workflows-and-jobs) on A reuses its successful deploy, so the new freshness check never executes. Smoke expects A and fails against B; rollback selects P because B lacks successful smoke (confirmed with the selector), then overwrites B. Retrying A’s rollback alone also bypasses the check — Independently guard rollback against superseded deployments and gate smoke retries, or reject rerun attempts in these jobs and require a fresh dispatch.
