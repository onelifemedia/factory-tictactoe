## Verdict
AGREE-WITH-CONCERNS

The approach is sound, but deployment scheduling and rollback selection need fixes to satisfy R-014.

## Findings
- [C1] [HIGH] F-013, concurrency criterion — The specified concurrency policy can discard the latest commit’s deployment — While deployment A runs, latest-main deployment C can become pending; older B’s CI then finishes and its deployment replaces C, only to skip because B is stale. `cancel-in-progress: false` does not prevent pending-run replacement. [GitHub documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/how-tos/write-workflows/choose-when-workflows-run/control-workflow-concurrency) — Explicitly enable queued concurrency (`queue: max`), retain the stale-SHA check after acquiring the deployment slot, and test this completion order.
- [C2] [HIGH] F-013, rollback criterion — “Latest successful run” does not establish that its deployment and smoke test succeeded — A stale-commit run can successfully perform its eligibility check and skip deployment, leaving no Pages artifact; selecting it prevents rollback despite an earlier valid artifact. Skipped jobs do not necessarily make a workflow fail. [GitHub documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/how-tos/write-workflows/choose-when-workflows-run/control-jobs-with-conditions) — Require evidence of successful deployment and smoke testing when selecting the previous run; test a successful no-op run between the last good deployment and the failed deployment.
- [C3] [MEDIUM] F-004, second acceptance criterion — It unconditionally requires a computer reply after an empty-square human move — The final X in the design’s sample draw fills the board, leaving no legal O move; A4 and A5 also explicitly end after X — Require terminal evaluation immediately after X, and call the opponent only if play continues; assert the opponent is not called for human wins or human-completed draws.
- [C4] [MEDIUM] F-013, manual trigger — The plan introduces `workflow_dispatch` without specifying its deployment eligibility — Architecture §6 requires manual runs to resolve main’s tip and verify successful CI for that exact SHA, whereas the plan only specifies `workflow_run.head_sha` — Add separate SHA resolution and CI verification criteria for manual runs, including refusal when main’s CI is pending or failed.

## Missing
- F-013 depends only on F-012, whose dependency chain excludes F-008–F-011. Add dependencies on F-010 and F-011, or an explicit enforced M3 completion gate, so deployment cannot precede keyboard, accessibility, announcement and fallback completion.
