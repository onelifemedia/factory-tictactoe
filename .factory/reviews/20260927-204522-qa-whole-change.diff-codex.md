## Verdict
AGREE-WITH-CONCERNS

Partial job reruns can bypass the deployment freshness check and overwrite a newer release.

## Findings
- [C1] [HIGH] `.github/workflows/deploy.yml:104–115` — The freshness gate runs only in `prepare`, so it does not protect a retried `deploy` job — Scenario: A passes prepare/build but deployment fails; B subsequently deploys successfully; “Re-run failed jobs” on A reuses its successful prerequisites and deploys A without checking main again. GitHub supports these [partial reruns](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/how-tos/manage-workflow-runs/re-run-workflows-and-jobs), violating R-014’s stale-run guarantee — Revalidate the candidate inside the deployment job on every attempt and gate downstream smoke/rollback accordingly, or reject rerun attempts and require a fresh dispatch. Add this A/B/retry regression case.
